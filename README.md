I am an Electrical & Computer Engineering senior working at the intersection of trustworthy AI, signal processing, and developer infrastructure.

Engineering Principles

Trust should be designed into the architecture, not added as a label afterward.

Research tooling should make experiments reproducible, comparable, and difficult to misuse.

AI agents need explicit permissions and observable actions before they can be trusted with engineering tools.

Open-source projects should explain their value quickly and let the implementation prove the rest.
